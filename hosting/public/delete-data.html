Delete your TankMind data

TankMind by Hanif Tech

TankMind doesn't have user accounts. Almost everything you enter is stored only on your device, and the little that reaches our servers is tied to a random anonymous ID and deletes itself after 30 days.

Data on your device

Tanks, logs, livestock, plants, reminders, photos and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them:

  • Delete individual items inside the app, or
  • Clear the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → TankMind → Storage → Clear storage), or
  • Uninstall TankMind.

Backup files you exported yourself are stored wherever you saved them (for example Google Drive); delete those files there.

Data on our servers

If you used the AI assistant or bought Premium, our servers hold:

  • Daily AI usage counters linked to your anonymous ID
  • Technical logs of AI requests (time, model, errors)
  • Purchase records held by RevenueCat for subscription status

Usage counters and logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. We do not store your AI conversations.

Request deletion now

  1. Open TankMind → Settings → About → Support ID and tap it to copy.
  2. Email us with the subject "Delete my data" and paste your Support ID.
Email a deletion request

Or write to kkssoftwaresolutions@gmail.com. We delete the usage counters, logs and purchase-service records linked to that Support ID within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Without the Support ID we can't identify which anonymous records are yours. If you no longer have the app installed, the records expire on their own within 30 days. To stop a subscription, cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or App Store settings; store purchase records are kept by Google or Apple under their policies.
