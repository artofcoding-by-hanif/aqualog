TankMind Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

TankMind is an aquarium management app published by Hanif Tech ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have.

The short version
  • Your tanks, logs, livestock, plants, reminders and photos are stored on your device. We don't upload them to our servers.
  • We don't ask for your name, email or phone number, and there's no sign-up.
  • When you use the AI assistant or health check, a summary of the selected tank and your messages are sent to our server and to Google's Gemini service to generate the answer. We don't store your conversations, but Google may use them to improve its products (see section 2). Don't include personal information in your questions.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store through RevenueCat. We never see your payment card.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no selling of data.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in TankMind is saved in the app's private storage on your phone: tank details, water test readings, livestock, plants, equipment, hardscape, maintenance and feeding logs, reminders and app settings. Tank photos you take with the camera or choose from your gallery are copied into the app's storage.

This data stays on your device. It is removed when you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall TankMind.

Backups. When you tap Backup Data, the app creates a file containing your data and photos and opens your device's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it (for example Google Drive or your Files app). Before a restore, the app keeps a copy of your current data on the device.

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device and are not sent to us.

2. Data that leaves your device

Anonymous app identity

The first time you use an online feature (the AI assistant or a purchase), the app signs in to Google Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random identifier that isn't linked to your name, email or Google account. We use it to apply daily usage limits and to connect purchases to your app. Firebase records when the identifier was created and last used and, like any online service, processes your IP address to provide and protect the service. Settings in the app shows this identifier as your Support ID.

App integrity checks

To stop other apps from misusing our AI service, the app uses Firebase App Check, which asks Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest (iOS) to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of TankMind. These services receive information about your device and app installation needed to make that check.

AI assistant and health check

When you send a chat message or run a health check, the app sends to our server (Google Cloud Functions):

  • Your chat messages in the current conversation (for chat), and
  • A summary of the tank you selected: name, type, size, setup date, recent water test readings, livestock, plants, filters, lighting, substrate, hardscape, recent maintenance and feeding entries, and open reminders.

The summary does not include photos, file locations or internal record numbers. Our server forwards this information to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer and returns the answer to your device. Google processes this data under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.

TankMind currently uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use the content of AI requests (your messages, the tank summary and the AI's answers) to provide, improve and develop its products and services, and human reviewers may read it. Google does not receive your name or contact details from us, only the request content. Don't include personal or sensitive information in your questions. If you prefer not to share this content with Google, don't use the AI assistant or health check; the rest of the app works without them.

We do not store your messages, the AI's replies or your tank summary on our servers. Chat history lives on your device for as long as the app is open, and the latest health report is saved on your device. Our server keeps only:

  • A daily request counter per anonymous identifier, used to apply free and Premium limits. Counters are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Technical logs (anonymous identifier, time, which AI model answered, how long it took and any error) to keep the service working. Logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Subscriptions and purchases

If you buy TankMind Premium, the payment is processed by Google Play or the App Store under their own terms; we don't receive your card or billing details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and manage your subscription status. RevenueCat receives your anonymous identifier, your purchase and subscription history (products, dates, renewals, store transaction IDs), your country or currency, and basic device and app information. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your identifier has an active Premium subscription.

Fonts

The app downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts the first time it runs. Like any web request, this shares your IP address and device type with Google. See Google Fonts privacy.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don't access your contacts, location or microphone.
  • We don't use your data to build profiles or train our own models.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Use TankMind offline. Everything except the AI assistant and purchases works without sending data to us.
  • Delete your on-device data by deleting it in the app, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.
  • Delete server-side data. Usage counters and logs delete themselves after 30 days. To have them deleted sooner, email us your Support ID (Settings → About → Support ID). Without it we can't tell which anonymous records are yours. Step-by-step instructions: Delete your TankMind data.
  • Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Google Play or App Store settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise them.

6. Children

TankMind is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the AI assistant and want related records deleted, contact us with the Support ID.

7. Security

Data sent to our server travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our AI service only accepts requests from genuine copies of the app, and our API keys are kept on the server, not in the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so keep regular backups.

8. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its store listing.
