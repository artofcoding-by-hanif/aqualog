## Full description (max 4000)

AquaLog is the all-in-one companion for fishkeepers. Log every tank, water test and water change, keep track of your fish, shrimp and plants, and get personalised advice from a built-in AI assistant that actually knows your aquarium.

🐠 EVERY TANK IN ONE PLACE
• Freshwater, planted, saltwater, reef, brackish, shrimp and pond setups
• Volume calculator from tank dimensions
• Photo gallery and a cover photo for each tank
• See setup date, age and whether a tank is cycled at a glance

💧 WATER QUALITY, LOGGED AND CHARTED
• Record temperature, pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, GH, KH, phosphate and more
• Trend charts with ideal-range bands so problems stand out early
• Water change and maintenance history with percentages and notes

🐟 LIVESTOCK, PLANTS AND EQUIPMENT
• Fish and invertebrates with quantities, dates added and status
• Plants, filters, lighting schedules, rocks, driftwood and substrate layers
• Feeding log to keep portions and routines consistent

⏰ NEVER MISS A WATER CHANGE
• Reminders for water changes, tests, feeding, filter cleaning and more
• Overdue tasks highlighted on your dashboard
• A timeline of everything that happened in each tank

✨ AQUA AI ASSISTANT
• Ask anything about your tank and get answers based on its own readings, livestock, plants, equipment and maintenance history
• AI Health Check gives each tank a health score, explains what it found and builds a step-by-step action plan
• Clear, practical advice with livestock welfare first

🛠 HANDY TOOLS
• Glass thickness calculator for DIY tanks
• Filter calculator to size flow for your volume

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Works offline; your tanks and photos are stored on your device
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Back up everything to a single file and restore it anytime

⭐ AQUALOG PREMIUM (optional)
Free includes 2 tanks, 3 AI requests a day and 30 days of charts. Premium unlocks:
• Unlimited tanks
• Up to 100 AI requests a day
• Full chart history, multi-parameter overlays and CSV export

Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime in Google Play.

AI answers can be wrong. Always double-check advice that affects your livestock.
